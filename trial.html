How a project runs

The same four steps whether it is a small fix or a full product.

  1. Talk

    A free working session to understand the problem, the people and the budget.

  2. Plan

    A written scope, timeline and fixed price or clear estimate.

  3. Build

    Short cycles with regular demos, so you see progress and can steer.

  4. Support

    Launch, handover, training and ongoing care when you want it.
